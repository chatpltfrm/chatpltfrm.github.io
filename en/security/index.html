Security for organizations that handle sensitive data

Meets the requirements of Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law

  • Data stored on servers in Indonesia

    Messages, files, and account data for the Cloud service are stored and processed on servers located in the Republic of Indonesia.

  • No AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure – Telegraf uses a local cloud provider

    Telegraf is hosted by a local cloud provider in Indonesia, not on AWS, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure.

  • Telegraf is subject to Indonesian law

    As an Indonesian company, PT. Chat Platform Indonesia is subject to Indonesian law, including Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection. Because the data is not hosted by a US cloud provider, it is not under the control of a provider subject to the CLOUD Act.

  • No use of external tracking tools

    Telegraf does not use any external tracking tools, such as Google Analytics or IP address based services. The data collected is processed exclusively by Telegraf.
